Yeah. This is another interesting coaster as its currently the only family sit down B&M.

Jason rides another B&M.

So one cool thing is that Penguin Trek is another launched B&M, with a launched lifthill. Nice little addition to the ride.

So one thing I've been hearing from A LOT of enthusiasts is that Penguin Trek, and nearly ALL the new B&Ms built these past few years have been EXTREMELY rough. Now I can't speak for the adult B&Ms that have been getting rattle complaints as I haven't ridden any of those (Iron Menace, Wrath of Rakshasa, Tormenta, etc.) and officially comment on any of those. But Penguin Trek? I had NO problems with it. I didn't notice ANY sort of rattle at all. And it's not just me. Jason, who is FAR more sensitive to roughness, also never complained about a rattle or it feeling off in any way. So......I have no idea where these roughness and rattle complaints came from on Penguin Trek. Also, I know it doesn't get rattle complaints, but Pipeline here, is a new B&M, and I had no problems with that (more on Pipeline down below). I'm not sure if we were just lucky, or if other enthusiasts are just weaker than I am (I'd just come to that conclusion, if it wasn't for the fact that Jason also never felt a rattle).

Debunking roughness complaints aside, Penguin Trek is a fine family coaster.

Yeah. It may be far from the best ride ever, or even the best family coaster ever (Florida is honestly the king for stand out family coasters). But I still had fun on it and would recommend Penguin Trek to young coaster enthusiasts and those with a low coaster tolerance.

And just like with how you can still see the Arctic exhibition after riding Arctic Rescue @ SWSD (BTW, Arctic Rescue > Penguin Trek), it's the same thing with the penguin exhibition and Penguin Trek.
