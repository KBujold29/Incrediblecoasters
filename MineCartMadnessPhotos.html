Mine-Cart Madness Review

We're here at Epic Universe, where today, we'll be reviewing Mine-Cart Madness. This is......an interesting ride. It looks like......those Donkey Kong tracks where you ride in the mine-cars and jump off the traxk. Yeah. They actually made thuis in real life! And.....it looks COOL!!! Especially since there's no other coaster on Earth like it. Well, except for the clone at Universal Studios Japan (I'm overdue to return to that park). So yeah. We're riding that jump the tracks mine cart from Donkey Kong! AWESOME!!! After waiting in WAY too long of a line, we hop in the cars, pull down the lapbar, and we're off! We roll around the turn, Donkey Kong music with the sound of some screech from Diddy Kong. We make our way up to a ramp. We climb up the ramp lifthill. We reach the top and.....there's no track! Oh wait. Yeah. I can see the hidden track. The track jumping with the decoy Mine Cart track theming. Looks GREAT in the ride. The illusion 100% works. On the ride? You can tell. You never feel like you're actually flying off the track. We roll down the track and into the temple. We bump into Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong on a mine cart and dive right off the track. We head down a curved drop, gaining speed, before jumping back on the track. We head up a hill, popping out of the temple. We gothrough some straight traxk. Dip down, go off a small ramp, flying ff the track only to land back on it. Looks great outside! But yeah. On the ride, the illusion is gone. We land back on the track, go around a turn, and into a buikding. Oh boy. I see more wackiness in here. Oh boy. The bridge here is broken. No worries. Diddy Kong will just leap from bioken section to broken section. Amazingly, we make it each and every time. We reach the end, hit the brakes, and turn out of the building. We climb up another lifthill. Reach the top, and......Bowser already destroyed the track. Is that minor little inconvienence stop us? F*CK NO!!! We fall off te broken track. CLIFF JUMP!!! Luckily, we fall onto more track and we gracefully land without even a hint of a THUD!!! I guess physics doesn't exist in the Mario Universe, We head up the track (look out for the gaps in it), going up the hill and into some straight track. We head down a curve with......who the hell designed this track? Cause between all the gaps, they sure as hell did a piss poor job. Bad Donkey Kong! Anyways, we gracefully clear all the gaps and at the end of the track. The track ends at the water and continues on the other side of the body of water. I guess Donkey Kong was gonna build a bridge here, and then just got lazy. No need to worry, as our mine cart just rolls on water, Jesus style. We roll back on the track, up a small hill, and into the brake run. We roll into the cave. Going through a dark ride portion, we see Donkey Kong.....bend down and smack the ground? Anyways, we roll on by, and Diddy Kong has officially gained the ultimate prize. BANANA!!! All this trouble for something you can get at the grocery store for a buck. Oh well. So yeah. That's Mine Cart Madness. And....it's just a really fun ride. Yeah. It's true that the gimmick doesn't really work on the ride. Especially during the big drops with an extended period of track jumping. You REALLY notice the hidden track there. But you know what? It works beautifully off the ride. And honestly, I'm just impressed they tried this at all and got it as good as they did. I think I remember xeeing the concept art for this years ago for the announcement of the Japanese version, thinking it was just a pipe dream. But.....they did it. Plus, even if you stripped it nacked, it'd still be a fun ride. Sp yeah. I do recommend this ride. Just be warned about the horrendous crowds this ride gets.

7/10

Location: Universal Orlando Resort (Epic Universe)

Opened: 2025

Built by: Setpoint

Last Ridden: August 18, 2025
