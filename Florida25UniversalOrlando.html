So whats most notable about this ride is that its the first coaster in the world (excluding the original Mine-Cart Madness at Universal Studios Japan) to give the illusion of jumping the track, in what's called a Boom Coaster.

While I do LOVE the concept, it looks GREAT offride, and I'm super happy they attempted this at all. However, on the ride.....you can tell. You can see the "hidden" track that keeps it safe. So the illusion, while effective off ride, just never sticks while on the ride.

But on top of such a cool gimmick, it's just a decent family coaster as well. Big thumbs up. I loved this ride.
