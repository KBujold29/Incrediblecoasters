So this is the fairs spinning coaster. It's imported straight from the European Fair Circuit, and......this was a really good one.

This one actually has a semi-unique layout. Sure, it's primarily the standard spinning mouse layout. But that banked curve is a nice touch that I really think improves the ride.

Another thing that makes this one stand out being that it spins the entire way, not just in the 2nd half. And this one really spins a lot. So yeah. Big thumbs up for Heidi.

Hey look! They have Techno Power here. I know it's not a top tier fair ride, but it still is a ton of fun and better than I remember.

Moving on and......oh boy! The booster here is even bigger than Speed!

Unfortunately, this was another example of bigger doesn't always mean better. As this one felt....slower. Not nearly as fast and snappy as Speed. Though luckily, they have that here as well (Yes, it's weird that in this case, the smaller one is the more intense version).

And since its a modern classic, Rockit is also present at the O.C. Fair. Not my favorite, but still a fun flat ride.
